Tone

Read-only view of the amp’s edit buffer. It follows knob turns on the amp and re-reads after a preset change. Editing arrives in P2.

Presets

Names as stored on the amp, in banks of 4. The current preset is known only after the amp reports a preset change (turn the preset knob). Selecting from the app is coming in P3.

Current preset: unknown until the amp reports a change.

Tuner

The tuner was switched on at the amp.

Engaging the tuner sets a live DSP flag on the amp; it is not a stored setting.

Switch on “Byte log” to record tx/rx bytes, decoded per layer (IAP, RSMC frames, McBsp, stream segments).
